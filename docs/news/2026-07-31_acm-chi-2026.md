# ApeiroRA presents at ACM CHI 2026

On April 14, 2026, we attended the [Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI)](https://dl.acm.org/conference/chi) in Barcelona. As part of the [Association for Computing Machinery (ACM)](https://www.acm.org/), CHI is the flagship conference of the [Special Interest Group on Computer–Human Interaction (SIGCHI)](https://sigchi.org/). We presented our [paper](https://xr4ce-chi26.tech-experience.at/papers/XR4CE_paper_17.pdf) at the workshop [XR for Challenging Environments: Enabling Human Performance and Agency under Stress (XR4CE)](https://xr4ce-chi26.tech-experience.at/).

The workshop brought together researchers and practitioners exploring the use of Extended Reality (XR) in demanding and high-stakes environments, including i.e. infrastructure management, robotics, and datacenter operations. A recurring theme across the presented work was the evolution of XR from an immersive visualization technology toward a robust support system designed to enhance data-driven decision-making under pressure. The discussion emphasized the need for AI systems to communicate uncertainty and reliability while preserving human agency. To achieve the objective of reliable human-machine interaction, discussions focused on reducing cognitive workload through context-aware interfaces that provide relevant information in a clear and actionable manner. In the context of immersive interfaces, digital twins were identified as responsive platforms that integrate and process real-time data to support informed decision-making. In addition, resilient fallback strategies were proposed to strengthen operational continuity and maintain reliable system performance under varying conditions.

For the first part of the workshop, we presented our accepted paper, [XR for Complex Datacenter Environments: Why XR Needs AAS and AI-Driven Interaction to Support Operators in Next-Generation Datacenters](https://xr4ce-chi26.tech-experience.at/papers/XR4CE_paper_17.pdf). The paper contributed a distinct perspective: effective XR solutions rely on advanced interaction design and the availability of reliable, consistent, and trustworthy operational data. The Asset Administration Shell (AAS) serves as a key enabling technology by providing standardized templates, submodels, and property descriptions based on IEC 61360. Moreover, the paper argues that, in mission-critical scenarios, context-aware AI assistance is essential to support timely and informed decision-making. At the same time, our submission emphasizes the need for adaptive human-machine collaboration while preserving situational awareness and decision authority. The later part of the workshop focused on identifying required research breakthroughs and associated challenges relevant to the [Virtual Worlds Strategic Research and Innovation Agenda (SRIA)](https://www.virtualworldsassociation.eu/actions/strategic-research-innovation-agenda-virtual-worlds-eu) for the European Union.

Overall, our paper strengthened ApeiroRA's visibility within the international XR community and provided an opportunity to engage with researchers and innovators from industry and academia.
